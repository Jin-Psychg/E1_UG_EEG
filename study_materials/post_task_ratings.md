# Author-developed post-task ratings

The same three items were administered after each experiment. The task displayed a visual-analogue scale anchored at 0% (*Überhaupt nicht*), 50% (*Neutral*), and 100% (*Vollständig*), with the marker initialized at 50%. The German item wording is verbatim; the English text is a faithful gloss.

| Rating | Verbatim German item | English gloss |
|---|---|---|
| 1, perceived facial influence | *Inwieweit glauben Sie, dass das Foto des Gegenübers Ihre Entscheidung über die Vorschläge beeinflusst hat?* | To what extent do you believe that the counterpart's photograph influenced your decision about the offers? |
| 2, offer-source credibility | *Inwieweit glauben Sie, dass die Vorschläge, auf die Sie gerade reagiert haben, von echten Menschen/Individuen gemacht wurden?* | To what extent do you believe that the offers to which you just responded were made by real people/individuals? |
| 3, face-source credibility | *Inwieweit glauben Sie, dass die standardisierten Fotos auf echten Menschen/Individuen basieren?* | To what extent do you believe that the standardized photographs were based on real people/individuals? |

The item text and response-scale implementation also appear in each experiment's `experiment.sce` file.
